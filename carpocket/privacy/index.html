Privacy Policy

Car Pocket Puzzle: Parking Jam (“Car Pocket”, the “App”), Android package com.riftlab.carpocket, published by Rift Lab (“we”, “us”).
Effective date: October 22, 2026 · https://riftlabstudio.github.io/carpocket/privacy/

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or any account. The App has no login.
  • Your game progress (levels, coins, settings, daily rewards) is saved only on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob and offers an optional “Remove Ads” purchase through Google Play. These Google services collect some device data as described below.

2. Data we collect directly

None. Rift Lab does not operate servers for the App and does not receive or store your personal data. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

3. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and rewarded ads) may collect the advertising ID, device and app information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and (with consent) personalize ads and prevent fraud. How Google uses data in ads.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent form where required (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland) and stores your choice on the device.
  • Google Play Billing processes the optional “Remove Ads” purchase. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see them.
  • Google Play In-App Review may show Google’s rating dialog.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (Google; planned for a future update) would collect app usage events, crash logs, device model, OS version and app instance identifiers to understand gameplay and fix crashes. This policy will be updated if their use changes.

Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Data is processed by Google and may be transferred to and stored in other countries, including the United States.

4. Purposes and legal bases

Showing ads and keeping the free game funded (consent for personalized ads where required by law, legitimate interest otherwise); processing purchases (performance of a contract); crash and usage analytics (legitimate interest / consent where required).

5. Your choices

  • Change ad consent at any time from the privacy/consent option in the App’s settings (where available) or reset/delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Google › Ads.
  • Buying “Remove Ads” stops banner and interstitial ads.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes all locally saved game data.

6. Retention and security

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Data held by Google is retained according to Google’s policies. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

7. Children

The App is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA). If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Your rights (GDPR, KVKK, CCPA)

EEA/UK (GDPR): you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing, and complain to your supervisory authority.
Turkey (KVKK, Law No. 6698): under Article 11 you may learn whether your data is processed, request information, correction or deletion, learn of third parties to whom data is transferred, and object; you may also apply to the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Kurumu).
California (CCPA/CPRA): you may know, delete and correct personal information and opt out of “sale” or “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information for money; ad personalization can be limited through the consent form and Android ad settings. We will not discriminate against you for exercising rights.
Because we hold no personal data ourselves, most requests are fulfilled by Google’s tools; we will gladly assist at the address below.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; material changes will be noted in the App or store listing.
